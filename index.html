Meet your DJ

Mark Anthony, aka Real2it

Mark Anthony has been in the music scene since 1999, and it shows in the transitions. One genre melts into the next so smoothly that the floor never gets a chance to empty. Country into classic rock, Motown into modern hits, cumbia into line dances. He reads the room and follows it.

Because here is the thing. It is not just about playing music. It is about making sure you and your guests have a great experience. Music sets the tone for the whole event, and Mark Anthony treats your night like it is the only one on the calendar.

By day, Mark Anthony works in the education sector as an administrator, and at home he is a family man first. He earned a doctorate degree along the way, though he would rather talk about your playlist than his credentials. By night, he is behind the decks.

  • One DJ, start to finish. The DJ you book is the DJ at your event.
  • Every age, every genre. If your guests love it, it is in the mix.
